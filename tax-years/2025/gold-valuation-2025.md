# Valuing Physical Gold in EUR for Box 3 – 2025 (Draft)

This page explains how to calculate the euro value of physical gold (coins or bars) for your Dutch **Box 3** tax return as of **1 January 2025**, using published reference data accepted by the Belastingdienst.

## 🧾 Use the annual tax valuation table

Dutch investors holding physical precious metals can use the official prices published in an annual PDF (commonly from **Holland Gold**) titled:

> **Belastingaangifte Edelmetaal 2025**

This document typically provides euro values for common gold and silver coins/bars based on the **LBMA fixing** of the last business day of 2024 (the business day before the 1 January reference date, *peildatum*). These values are pre-converted to euros using the **ECB exchange rate**, so **no additional currency conversion is needed**.

## 🧮 How to use it in your tax return

1. **Count** how many coins or bars you hold.
2. **Multiply** by the matching euro value from the table.
3. Report the result in Box 3 under **“Overige bezittingen – edelmetalen”**.
4. No further FX conversion or appraisal needed.

✅ These values already include:
- The correct **LBMA gold price**
- The **ECB exchange rate** for USD/EUR on the last business day of 2024
- The mandatory “omrekenkoers” adjustment required by the Belastingdienst

## ⚠️ Notes & caveats

- These values reflect **bullion/melt value** only — collector premiums are ignored for tax purposes.
- If your metal is stored with a different provider, ensure it matches the listed specifications (e.g., weight, purity).
- The PDF carries the usual disclaimer that no legal rights can be derived from it — but it has been widely accepted by the Dutch tax authority over many years.
